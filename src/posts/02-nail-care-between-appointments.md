Great nails don't end when you leave the chair. A few easy moves keep your set gorgeous between visits: cuticle oil daily (the single best habit), gloves for dishes, and SPF on your hands — yes, even in LA, especially in LA.

Notice a little lift or a chipped tip? Don't pick — book a quick fix. A five-minute repair beats starting over, and it protects your natural nail underneath.
